Daily Streak Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Daily Streak Habit Tracker collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

Effective date: April 11, 2026
App model: Free with ads

Daily Streak Habit Tracker respects your privacy. We collect only the information needed to provide account access, sync your habit data, support reminders, and keep the app running reliably.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name and email address
  • Authentication details handled through Firebase Authentication
  • Habit data you create, including habits, streaks, reminders, notes, logs, and settings
  • Device and app information related to notifications, performance, and advertising
  • Advertising identifiers used by Google AdMob

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Store and sync your habits and progress
  • Send reminder notifications you enable in the app
  • Provide streak tracking, calendars, and statistics
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob

3. Authentication

The app supports email/password login and Google Sign-In. User authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Your habit data is associated with your account so it can be accessed when you sign in.

5. Notifications

The app may send reminder notifications that you enable inside the app. These notifications are used only for habit tracking and related engagement.

6. Advertising

Daily Streak Habit Tracker is free and supported by ads. The app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.

Google and its partners may use device identifiers and similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be processed by trusted third-party providers used to operate the app, including Google Firebase and Google AdMob.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account remains active or as needed to provide app functionality. If you delete your account, your associated app data is deleted from our systems, subject to technical or legal retention requirements.

9. Your Choices

  • Update or delete habits inside the app
  • Disable notifications from your device settings
  • Delete your account from the profile/settings section of the app

10. Children’s Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
